# Product

Gamernes Gamer is the companion site for a recurring gaming tournament between a
small group of friends. Each season the participants pick games, compete across
them for points, and crown a winner.

## What the site is for

- **Primary: post-tournament.** A permanent monument — stats, career records,
  champion history and "remember when" content. Tournaments run every 6–12 months
  and last 2 days, so the site is a monument for ~363 days and a live companion
  for ~2.
- **Secondary: during the tournament.** Live leaderboard and results tracking.
- **The group chat is the social layer, not the site.** The site produces
  shareable artifacts (champion cards, podium graphics, stat cards) for the chat.
  No comments, reactions, notifications or public accounts.

## Users

- **Participants** (primary) — the 4–5 competitors, reliving seasons and
  following the live event.
- **Spectators** (secondary) — a small but real audience.

## Constraints that shape features

- Hobby project: fun and polish matter as much as utility.
- New seasons recur — adding a season is a core, repeated workflow.
- The roster changes between seasons. Never assume a fixed set of players.
- Power-ups and curses vary per season. Model them per season, not globally.
